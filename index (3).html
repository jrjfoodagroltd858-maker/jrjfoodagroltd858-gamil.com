Corporate Profile

Leading Agro Processing with Integrity & Industrial Purity

JRJ FOOD & AGRO INDUSTRIES LTD is a dynamic agro-processing enterprise operating in Bangladesh since 2025. We specialize in the large-scale extraction, refining, and supply of coconut-derived oils and by-products tailored for consumer goods, cosmetic formulations, and industrial livestock feed.

With modern manufacturing operations based in Godnail, Shiddirganj, Narayanganj and our Dhaka Sales Office located at Sagupta D Lorel, Kamalapur Bazar Road, we maintain an unbroken supply chain serving over 1,000,000 wholesale touchpoints across Bangladesh.

Our Mission

Mission Statement

To supply premium-quality coconut oil products using modern technology, maintaining standards and supporting long-term partnerships with corporate, industrial, and institutional buyers.
